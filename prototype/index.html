US drones: what is changing, and what to go ask

A desk-research primer for the opening phase of an industry inquiry, built to earn the right to ask good questions before the interviews start. It sets out the flag we are planting, the forces moving the market, the sub-spaces worth a closer look, what we currently believe and what would change our minds, and who could tell us. Facts link to a source. Judgements are marked “our read”. The two never share a line.

01

The flag

Where we are starting, stated concretely enough to act on and loosely enough to be wrong. Held firmly enough to guide the first outreach, revised as the interviews come in.

02

Forces of change

Dated, sourced events behind the claim that this is a market in motion. Each is tagged as an internal change (inside the industry: buyers, rules, rivals) or an external one (war, technology, geopolitics), because the most interesting openings tend to sit where the two collide.

03

Sub-spaces

The territory, cut into segments. Scope stays here; attention can drop into any one of these for a closer look and climb back out. Names marked ↗ open in the constellation.

04

Hypotheses

What we currently believe, with the evidence on both sides. This whole section is argument: each evidence line is a sourced fact plus how we read it, and confidence is our judgement from desk research alone. None of it has met an interview yet. A hypothesis that gains weight should do so from several independent sources, unprompted.

05

Who to learn from

A starting target list across vantage points, chosen for what each could teach rather than for how senior they are. Start closer to the work: operators and program staff educate; executives confirm. Public roles and published work only.

06

Questions to take in

What desk research could not answer. Anything a search engine can answer has been answered above, so these are for people. Lenses, not a script: pick the one the conversation is already heading toward.

07

Where insiders talk

The watering holes: what to read, where to show up, and the public datasets that let you check a claim yourself.

08

Vocabulary

The words insiders use without explaining. The same definitions appear on hover wherever a term is used across all three views.

09

Research log

Dated, append-only. How the thinking moved, so the trail survives later edits.
